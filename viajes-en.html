Vocabulary

Viajes

Essential vocabulary for getting around, booking things, and handling problems when travelling in a Spanish-speaking country.

At the airport

el vuelo (flight), la maleta (suitcase), el equipaje de mano (carry-on), la tarjeta de embarque (boarding pass), facturar (to check in luggage), el mostrador (counter), la puerta de embarque (gate)

Accommodation

reservar (to book), la habitación doble/individual (double/single room), el hotel, el albergue (hostel), hacer el check-in / check-out, la recepción

Transport

el billete (Spain) / el boleto (Latin America) = ticket, ida y vuelta (round trip), el andén (platform), coger/tomar el autobús (to take the bus), alquilar un coche (to rent a car)

Asking for directions

¿Cómo se llega a...? (How do I get to...?) Siga todo recto (Go straight ahead). Gire a la derecha/izquierda (Turn right/left). Está a dos calles de aquí (It's two blocks from here).

Useful phrases

PhraseWhen to use it

¿Dónde está la parada más cercana?Asking for the nearest stop

Billete vs boleto: in Spain you say "billete" (train, plane ticket); in most of Latin America it's "boleto". Both are correct — it's a regional difference, not a mistake.
