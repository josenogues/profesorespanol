The letters that cause most trouble

If you can name these, half the phone calls are solved.

LetterNameWhy it causes trouble
b / vbe / uveThey sound exactly the same in Spanish: always spell them out.
g / jge / jota«ge» and «gi» sound like the jota.
c / zce / zeta«ce», «ci» and «za, zo, zu» sound alike in most of Spain.
hhacheIt is silent: you never hear it.
ll / ydoble ele / i griega (o ye)They sound the same for most speakers.
wuve doble (o doble uve)Common in emails and websites.
k / qka / cu«cu» is the letter q: don't mix it up with u.
r / rrerre / doble erreYou say «doble erre», not «dos erres».

Numbers, figures and dates

WhatHow it is said
ThousandsWith a full stop: 12.500 → doce mil quinientos (the opposite of English)
DecimalsWith a comma: 3,5 → tres coma cinco
Long codesDigit by digit or in pairs: 4471 → cuatro, cuatro, siete, uno / cuarenta y cuatro, setenta y uno
Phone numbersIn pairs: 02 345 67 89 → cero dos, tres cuatro cinco, sesenta y siete, ochenta y nueve
Zeros«cero», never «o»: 0071 → cero, cero, siete, uno
Datesel 12 de marzo / el día 12 → never «el 12 marzo» without «de»
Timesa las 19:30 → a las siete y media de la tarde / a las diecinueve treinta
Percentagesel 30 % → el treinta por ciento (with the article)
Quantities2 palés / 500 kg → dos palés, quinientos kilos
