Expressions

Modismos

Sayings and proverbs, closer to traditional wisdom than everyday conversation. Very useful for understanding older native speakers or informal contexts.

SayingMeaning
Más vale tarde que nuncaBetter late than never
No hay mal que por bien no vengaEvery cloud has a silver lining
A quien madruga, Dios le ayudaThe early bird catches the worm
En boca cerrada no entran moscasSometimes it's better to keep quiet
Dime con quién andas y te diré quién eresYou're known by the company you keep
Perro que ladra no muerdeHis bark is worse than his bite
Al mal tiempo, buena caraKeep a positive attitude even when things go wrong
Camarón que se duerme, se lo lleva la corrienteIf you snooze, you lose
Sayings vs everyday expressions: the ones on the "Expresiones comunes" page get used constantly in casual conversation; these sound more like proverbs — pulled out at a specific moment, almost like a moral. You don't need to memorize all of these, but recognizing them will help a lot with movies, shows, and older speakers.
