Vocabulary

Casa

Vocabulary for rooms, furniture, and household chores — very useful for describing where you live or looking for a place to rent.

Rooms

el salón (living room), la cocina (kitchen), el dormitorio/la habitación (bedroom), el baño (bathroom), el pasillo (hallway), la terraza/el balcón

Furniture

la mesa (table), la silla (chair), el sofá, la cama (bed), el armario (wardrobe), la estantería (shelf/bookcase)

Appliances

la nevera/el frigorífico (fridge), el horno (oven), la lavadora (washing machine), el lavavajillas (dishwasher), el microondas

Household chores

limpiar (to clean), cocinar (to cook), hacer la compra (to do the grocery shopping), poner/quitar la mesa (to set/clear the table), sacar la basura (to take out the trash), planchar (to iron)
