Spanish sounds, not English sounds

Six things that give an English speaker away, and the two places where the sound is the whole word. Read every example out loud — this is the one class that does not work in silence.

1. Five vowels, and you are probably sliding them

Spanish has five vowel sounds. Not five letters with several sounds each — five sounds, always the same, in every word, stressed or not.

you sayEnglish habitSpanish
nonou — it slides into a uone short o and stop
cafécafei — it slides into an ione short e and stop
MadridMuhdrid — the first a goes lazya full a, same as the other
bebétwo different e soundsthe same e twice
English vowels slide and go lazy. Spanish ones do neither. Every «o» is the o of sol, stressed or not. This one thing, fixed, takes more accent off you than all the consonants together.

2. The letters that are not there

Three places where you write a letter and say nothing at all.

letterruleexamples
halways silent, no exceptionshola · hombre · hay · ahora
u in que, quisilent — «ke», «ki»queso · quiero · quince
u in gue, guisilent — «ge», «gi» with a hard gguerra · guitarra
ü in güe, güinot silent — the two dots bring it backpingüino · vergüenza
That is what the two dots are for. Without them the u is a silent helper; with them it is a sound again. Pingüino really is «pin-gwee-no».

3. B and V are the same sound

Not similar. The same. There is no English «v» in Spanish at all.

sound
vaca (cow)baca (roof rack)identical
tuvo (he had)tubo (pipe)identical
vino (wine)bino —the v is a b
Do not put your teeth on your lip. A Spanish v is made with two lips, like a b. Spaniards cannot hear the difference either — that is why they ask ¿con be o con uve? when you spell a name.

4. The jota, and the g that becomes one

One sound — the scraped one at the back of the throat — written two different ways. This is the system behind half the spelling rules you have seen.

spellingsoundexamples
j + anythingalways the jotajamón · joven · traje
g + e, ithe jota toogente · girar · elegir
g + a, o, uhard g, like «go»gato · gota · gusto
gu + e, ihard g — the u is the shieldguerra · guitarra
Now the u in guerra makes sense. It is not decoration: it is there to stop the g turning into a jota. Take it out and guerra would be read «jerra».

5. One r or two — it is a different word

The only two consonants in Spanish where getting it wrong does not give you an accent, it gives you another word.

one r — a single taptwo r — rolled
pero butperro dog
caro expensivecarro cart
coro choircorro I run
pera pearperra female dog
And at the start of a word, one r is already the rolled one. Roma, rojo, ratón — written with one, said with the strong one. Same after n, l and s: Enrique, alrededor.

6. The tilde is not decoration

Same letters, different stress, different word. Nothing else changes.

no tildewith the tilde
hablo I speakhabló he spoke
canto I singcantó she sang
papa potatopapá dad
esta this oneestá he is
And one set of letters can be three words. Término (a term), termino (I finish), terminó (he finished) — same seven letters, three meanings, and the only difference is where you push. If you move the stress you have not mispronounced the word: you have said a different one. The rule for when a word carries a tilde is on the pronunciation page.
