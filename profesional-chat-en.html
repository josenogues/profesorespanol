Chat de trabajo (work chat)

Teams and Slack are neither an email nor a WhatsApp with friends: they're their own register, direct but careful. These are the differences that show.

What changes compared to email

The formulas that cover 90%

Asking

  • ¿Tienes un momento? — asking for a moment of their time
  • Cuando puedas, ¿me echas un ojo a esto? — asking without any urgency
  • ¿Te pillo bien? — is now a good time?

Flagging

  • Te paso el archivo. — announcing you're sending it
  • Ojo, que el enlace ha cambiado. — flagging something important
  • Ya está subido. — confirming it's done

Closing

  • Perfecto, lo miro. — acknowledging and taking it on
  • Genial, gracias. — closing with thanks
  • Hecho. — closing in one word

Mistakes that show

The bare «hola»

Hola. […]

Writing «hola» and waiting for a reply before saying what you want. It's the habit that frustrates people most. Say the greeting and the point in the same message.

The email in disguise

Estimada Marta: me dirijo a ti para...

Email register inside a chat sounds odd and distant.

The machine gun

eight one-line messages in a row

Every message is a notification for the other person. Put the idea in one.

The full stop: in chat, ending a short message with a period («Vale.») can read as annoyance. No period is the norm. In email, it's the other way round.
