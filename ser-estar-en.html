Essential irregular verbs: ser · estar · haber

These three verbs are at the heart of Spanish. Before diving into conjugations, here's what each one means:

ser — Permanent / Identity "to be"

Used for identity, origin, profession, and inherent characteristics.

estar — Temporary / State "to be"

Used for moods, conditions, location, and temporary states.

haber — Auxiliary "to have"

Used to form compound tenses. Also: hay = there is / there are.

Ser vs. Estar: Both mean "to be" but are never interchangeable. Ser is for identity (Soy médico — I am a doctor). Estar is for states and location (Estoy cansado — I am tired). Also note: hay (from haber) means "there is / there are" and is one of the most useful words in Spanish!
