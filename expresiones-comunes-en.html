Expressions

Expresiones comunes

Set phrases that native speakers use constantly in everyday conversation — they don't always translate literally.

ExpressionMeaning
No hay de quéReply to "thank you", like "you're welcome"
¡Qué va!Emphatic "no way!"
Estar en las nubesTo be distracted, "have your head in the clouds"
Dar en el clavoTo hit the nail on the head
Tomar el peloTo pull someone's leg / tease
Ponerse las pilasTo get your act together, step it up
Costar un ojo de la caraTo cost an arm and a leg
No tener pelos en la lenguaTo not mince words, speak bluntly
Meter la pataTo put your foot in it, make a blunder
Estar como una cabraTo be a bit crazy (affectionately)
How to practice them: don't translate word-for-word — learn each one as a complete block tied to a situation. Some map neatly onto an English idiom (like "an arm and a leg"), but most don't, so don't assume there's always a direct match.
