Common expressions

Set phrases that native speakers use constantly in everyday conversation — they don't always translate literally.

Very common expressions

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
No hay de qué You're welcomeUsed as an informal reply when someone thanks you
¡Qué va! No way!Used to strongly deny something someone just said
Estar en las nubes To have your head in the cloudsSaid of someone who isn't paying attention to what's going on
Dar en el clavo To hit the nail on the headUsed when someone gets something exactly right
Tomar el pelo To pull someone's legUsed when someone is playfully joking around with another person
Ponerse las pilas To get your act togetherUsed to push yourself or someone else to try harder
Costar un ojo de la cara To cost an arm and a legUsed to complain that something is very expensive
No tener pelos en la lengua To not mince wordsUsed to describe someone who always speaks their mind bluntly
Meter la pata To put your foot in itUsed when someone makes an embarrassing mistake
Estar como una cabra To be a bit crazyUsed affectionately to say that someone is a bit crazy

Greetings and goodbyes

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¡Qué alegría verte! So great to see you!Warm greeting when you run into someone
¿Qué tal todo? How's everything going?Informal way of asking how everything's going
¡Cuánto tiempo! It's been ages!"It's been ages!" — said after not seeing someone in a long time
Nos vemos See youInformal goodbye, "see you"
Que vaya bien Take careFarewell wishing someone well
Un abrazo / Un beso A hug / A kissAffectionate sign-off, typical when talking or writing to friends
Hasta la próxima Until next time"Until next time"

At the table

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¡Que aproveche! Enjoy your meal!Said before eating, "enjoy your meal"
¿Me pasas la sal? Can you pass the salt?Politely asking for something at the table
Está buenísimo/a It's deliciousSaying the food is delicious
Estoy lleno/a I'm fullSaying you can't eat any more
¿Repites? Want seconds?Offering or asking if someone wants seconds
¡Salud! Cheers!A toast before drinking
Se me hace la boca agua My mouth is wateringReally wanting to eat something, "my mouth is watering"

At work

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¿Cómo va todo por la oficina? How's everything at the office?Informal way to ask how work is going for someone
Se me ha hecho tarde I ended up running lateExplaining that you ended up running late
Estoy hasta arriba de trabajo I'm swamped with workHaving a ton of work, being swamped
¿Tienes un minuto? Got a minute?Asking a colleague for a bit of their time
Vamos a hacer una pausa Let's take a breakSuggesting a short break
¡Buen finde! Have a good weekend!Friday sign-off wishing someone a good weekend
Nos ponemos al día Let's catch upMeeting up to catch up on news

With friends and at the bar

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¿Qué te pongo? What can I get you?What the bartender asks when taking your order
¿Vamos a tomar algo? Shall we go for a drink?Suggesting going for a drink with friends
Ponme una caña Give me a small draft beerOrdering a small draft beer (typical in Spain)
Te invito yo It's on meOffering to pay for someone else
¡Va por ti! Cheers to you!A toast dedicated to someone
¿Nos tomamos otra? Shall we have another one?Suggesting having one more drink
Estamos de charla We're just chattingSaying you're hanging out chatting

Reacting: surprise, agreement and disagreement

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¡No me digas! No way! / You don't say!Expressing surprise at some news, "no way!"
¡Qué fuerte! That's crazy!Reacting to something shocking or over the top
Tienes toda la razón You're absolutely rightShowing full agreement with someone
Yo también lo creo I think so tooSaying you think the same thing
No estoy de acuerdo I disagreePolitely expressing disagreement
Depende It dependsAnswer when something isn't a clear yes or no
¡Ni hablar! No way, absolutely not!A flat refusal, "no way, absolutely not"

At the airport

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¿Dónde está el control de pasaportes? Where's passport control?Asking where passport control is
He perdido mi vuelo I've missed my flightExplaining that you missed your flight
¿Dónde recojo el equipaje? Where do I pick up my luggage?Asking where to pick up your luggage
Tengo algo que declarar I have something to declarePhrase used at customs
¿A qué hora embarca el vuelo? What time does the flight board?Asking the boarding time
Mi maleta no ha llegado My suitcase hasn't arrivedReporting lost luggage
¿Dónde está la puerta de embarque 12? Where's gate 12?Asking about a specific boarding gate

On public transport

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¿Dónde compro el billete? Where do I buy the ticket?Asking where to buy the ticket
¿Este autobús para en...? Does this bus stop at...?Asking if a bus stops at a specific place
¿Cuántas paradas quedan? How many stops are left?Asking how many stops are left
Próxima parada, por favor Next stop, pleaseAsking to be told when your stop comes
¿Dónde está la estación de metro más cercana? Where's the nearest metro station?Asking for the nearest metro station
¿Hace transbordo aquí? Do I transfer here?Asking if you need to transfer here
Un billete sencillo, por favor A one-way ticket, pleaseAsking for a one-way ticket

At the hotel

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
Tengo una reserva a nombre de... I have a reservation under the name...Checking in at reception
¿A qué hora es el check-out? What time is checkout?Asking about checkout time
¿El desayuno está incluido? Is breakfast included?Asking if breakfast is included
¿Me puede dar otra toalla, por favor? Could you give me another towel, please?Asking reception for something
La llave no funciona The key doesn't workExplaining a problem with the room
¿Tienen wifi gratis? Do you have free wifi?Asking about wifi
¿Podría guardar mi maleta hasta la tarde? Could you store my suitcase until the afternoon?Asking to store your luggage

At tourist attractions

ExpressionTranslationMeaning
¿Dónde puedo comprar las entradas? Where can I buy tickets?Asking where to buy tickets
¿Hay descuento para estudiantes? Is there a student discount?Asking about discounts
¿A qué hora cierra? What time does it close?Asking about closing time
¿Se pueden hacer fotos aquí? Can you take photos here?Asking if photos are allowed
¿Hay visitas guiadas en inglés? Are there guided tours in English?Asking about guided tours
¿Dónde están los baños? Where are the restrooms?Asking where the restrooms are
Nos hemos perdido, ¿nos puede ayudar? We're lost, can you help us?Asking for help when you're lost
How to practice them: don't translate word-for-word — learn each one as a complete block tied to a situation. Some map neatly onto an English idiom (like "an arm and a leg"), but most don't, so don't assume there's always a direct match.
