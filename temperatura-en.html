Expressions

Sobre temperatura

Talking about weather and temperature — watch out for hacer/estar/tener, three different verbs for three different situations.

Hacer + noun

Hace calor. Hace frío. Hace sol. Hace viento.

For general weather — literally "it makes hot/cold".

Estar + participle/adjective

Está nublado. Está lloviendo. Está nevando.

For specific sky conditions or ongoing actions.

Tener + noun Common trap

Tengo calor. Tengo frío.

For how a person feels — never "estoy calor" or "I am hot", always "tengo calor" (literally "I have heat").

Asking about temperature

SpanishMeaning
¿Qué tiempo hace hoy?What's the weather like today?
¿Qué temperatura hace?What's the temperature?
Hace 20 gradosIt's 20 degrees
Hace mucho calor / un poco de fríoIt's very hot / a bit cold
⚠️ The most common trap: English speakers naturally say "estoy caliente" thinking of "I am hot" — but in Spanish this has a sexual connotation! To say you feel hot (as a person), always use tengo calor.
