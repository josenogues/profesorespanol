Vocabulary

Trabajo

Workplace vocabulary: from job hunting to everyday office life.

Job hunting

el currículum (CV/resume), la oferta de trabajo (job posting), la entrevista (interview), contratar (to hire), el contrato, el sueldo/salario

At the office

la reunión (meeting), el jefe/la jefa (boss), el compañero/la compañera (colleague), el correo electrónico (email), el plazo (deadline), la tarea (task)

Types of schedule

a tiempo completo (full-time), a tiempo parcial (part-time), trabajar en remoto/teletrabajar (to work remotely), hacer horas extra (to work overtime), estar de baja (to be on sick leave)

Key verbs

solicitar (un puesto) = to apply for a job, renunciar / dimitir = to resign, ascender = to get promoted, despedir = to fire, jubilarse = to retire

Job interview phrases

PhraseUse
Tengo experiencia en...Talking about your background
Mi mayor fortaleza es...Answering "tell me about yourself"
¿Cuáles son las responsabilidades del puesto?Asking about the role
Estoy disponible a partir de...Talking about availability
False friend: "actualmente" does NOT mean "actually" (in reality/in fact) — it means "currently, right now". This is one of the most common false-friend slips in professional contexts: "Actualmente trabajo en..." = "I currently work at...", not "In reality I work at...".
