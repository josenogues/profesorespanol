Verbs

Overview

In Spanish, the same fact can be told in very different ways depending on the verb mood: it's not the same to state that something happens (indicative) as to doubt it, wish for it, or imagine it (subjunctive) — and giving a direct order (imperative) is different again. That subtlety is what trips up learners the most — and it's also what gives the language its nuance and expressive power. Here's the whole system, at a glance, with hablar as the example.

Futuro simple

Tiempos compuestos

Modo imperativo
Formas
