Overview

The building blocks of the Spanish verb system. Each block has its own dedicated page with full detail — this is just the map.

Regular vs. Irregular

Most verbs follow predictable patterns based on their ending (–AR, –ER, –IR). Common verbs like ser and ir are irregular — they must be memorized.

Three moods

Indicative (facts) — 10 verb tenses: 5 simple + 5 compound.
Subjunctive (doubts & desires) — 6 verb tenses: 3 simple + 3 compound.
Imperative (commands).

Non-finite forms

Infinitive (hablar), Gerund (hablando), Participle (hablado). These don't conjugate, and are used in compound tenses and periphrases.
