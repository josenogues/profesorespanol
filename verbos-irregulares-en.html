More irregular verbs

These high-frequency verbs are irregular but essential. Learn them and you'll unlock a huge portion of everyday Spanish conversation.

poder and querer are stem-changing verbs: the stem vowel changes in the present tense (puedo, quiero). This is a common pattern in Spanish — the stress shifts when the stem is stressed!
