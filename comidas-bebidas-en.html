Vocabulary

Comidas y bebidas

Essential food vocabulary — useful both at restaurants and for everyday grocery shopping.

Back to all topics

Meals of the day

el desayuno (breakfast), la comida/el almuerzo (lunch), la merienda (afternoon snack), la cena (dinner)

Basic foods

el pan (bread), el arroz (rice), la pasta (pasta), la carne (meat), el pescado (fish), las verduras (vegetables), la fruta (fruit), el huevo (egg), el queso (cheese)

Drinks

el agua (water), el vino (wine), la cerveza (beer), el café (coffee), el té (tea), el zumo/jugo (juice), el refresco (soda)

Flavors

dulce (sweet), salado (salty), picante (spicy), amargo (bitter), ácido (sour)

Tener hambre / tener sed

Just like with hot/cold, hunger and thirst use tener ("to have"), not "estar": Tengo hambre (I'm hungry, literally "I have hunger"), Tengo sed (I'm thirsty) — never "estoy hambre".
