The words that look English and aren't

Twenty-odd false friends, sorted by how much trouble they can get you into

1. The five that can actually embarrass you

Every one of these has been said in a real class by a real student. Learn these five first.

3. The short everyday ones

These aren't even Latin words pretending to be clever. They just happen to look like something else, and that makes them stick.

Around the house

  • ropa = clothes, not rope (cuerda)
  • sopa = soup, not soap (jabón)
  • carpeta = folder, not carpet (moqueta)

Out and about

  • librería = bookshop, not library (biblioteca)
  • largo = long, not large (grande)
  • once = eleven, not once (una vez)
A pattern worth knowing: the long Latin-looking words are usually safe — información, necesario, importante, posible all mean what you think. The traps are the short everyday ones. If a word is short and looks familiar, check it.
