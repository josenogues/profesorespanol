Verbos

Especial atención

Verbs that commonly trip people up, either because English doesn't distinguish them or because the construction is unfamiliar.

GUSTAR

  • Gustar = to like (literally "to be pleasing to")
  • Works backwards from English: the thing liked is the subject
  • Me gusta el café = coffee is pleasing to me
  • Me gustan los libros = the verb agrees with "books" (plural)
Practice this

PEDIR / PREGUNTAR

  • Pedir = to request an object or favor; preguntar = to ask a question
  • Pido un café → I ask for a coffee (request)
  • Pregunto la hora → I ask for the time (question)
Practice this

DEJAR / DEJAR DE

  • Dejar = to leave (an object, a place); dejar de + infinitive = to stop doing something
  • Dejo el libro aquí → I leave the book here
  • Dejo de fumar → I quit smoking
Practice this

RECORDAR / ACORDARSE (DE)

  • Recordar = no preposition needed; acordarse de = "de" is required
  • Recuerdo tu nombre → I remember your name
  • Me acuerdo de ti → I remember you
Practice this
